# The team

Who sings, plays, leads, preaches and runs the sound each Sunday, and who leads each
song. Once switched on, the community's bar has **Team → Team schedule** and
**My schedule**.

## The team schedule

![The team schedule](../screenshots/laptop-team-schedule.png)

Each weekly service and rehearsal has a template: so many vocalists, a guitarist, a
worship lead. Every date of the next 8 weeks starts from it; the team changes one date
without touching the others.

- **The team** puts someone in a place. They're told, accept or decline (saying why if
  they like), and a declined place opens again.
- **Open places** are offered to the people marked for that role; anyone else may offer,
  and the team confirms.
- **Away days**: on My schedule, mark the days you're away; the team sees them when it
  plans.

Owners rename, add or remove roles in **Settings → Roles**. A role for an instrument
counts the members who play it (from their musician profile). Mark the role that **leads
the songs**: the starter list's Worship lead does.

## My schedule

![My schedule](../screenshots/phone-my-schedule.png)

Your places with Accept and Decline, the open places you can take, and your away days.
An open place says **Take it** when you're among its role's people (it's yours at once),
else **Offer to do it** (the team confirms); point at it, or hold it on a phone, to read
why. Someone in several places of a date has the others named on each.

## Notifications

![Notifications](../screenshots/phone-notifications.png)

The bell in the bar shows how many things are new for you; tap it to read them, each
leading to its playlist or to your schedule. Switch on notifications there to get them on
your phone too: assigned, a reminder 3 hours before, open places, and the playlist's news.
On an iPhone, add Norless to the home screen first.

## Who leads each song

![Led by on the playlist](../screenshots/laptop-led-by.png)

Each song of a playlist is led by its service's worship lead. To give one to a singer of
that service, choose **Led by…** from the song's menu. The playlist row and the
musicians' and singers' views show who leads; the statistics remember it.

## The playlist is ready

When Sunday's playlist is done, choose **Tell the team it's ready** from the playlist's
menu: everyone in that Sunday's places is told, on their phone too. Songs changed after
that (chords, a key, a song added) are told to that Sunday's musicians and singers.

## The church's calendar

When an owner pasted the church's calendar in Settings → Schedule, My schedule and the
team schedule list what's coming in it: youth nights, conferences, weddings. It's only a
list: the services and their places stay Norless's own.
