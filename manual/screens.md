# Screens

For whoever sets up the projectors, the TVs and the live stream.

## Screens with their own link

**Settings → Screens** lists the room's screens: projectors, musicians' and singers'
screens, stage monitors and the live stream's overlay. Each has its languages, its
look (background, picture, text color, font, refrains in italics) and its own link. A
screen's link shows only what's live, without logging in. **New link** retires the old
one, for example after a TV was lent out.

![The Screens menu](../screenshots/laptop-screens-menu.png)

From a laptop, the **Screens** menu opens a screen full screen on a chosen display.

## Pairing a TV

A TV's browser doesn't need anyone to type a link:

1. On the TV, open `norless.com/pair`. It shows a short code and a QR code.
2. In the **Screens** menu, choose **Pair a device…**, type or scan the code, and pick the
   screen.
3. The TV shows that screen, and comes back to it after a power cut, until it's
   unpaired from the same menu.

![Pairing a TV](../screenshots/laptop-pair-a-tv.png)

## The live stream

A **broadcast overlay** screen shows the words as a lower third, two lines at a time,
with an outline that stays readable over the video. Its background is transparent (a
browser source in OBS), chroma green or black (for a video mixer).

![The broadcast overlay](../screenshots/screen-broadcast.png)

## Project here

**Project here**, on a song's page or a playlist, projects from the device you hold,
without logging in: a laptop opens a projector window, a phone fills its screen (tap
the right half for next, the left half for previous, Back to return).

## Following along on a phone

Anyone in the church can follow the live song on their own phone at your community's
address, `norless.com/<community>`, without an account: the whole song, the live part
in view, in one language or both, with a text size of their own. Owners can show a QR
code to it on the start page.

## Verses from bible.com

With Nicolae Matei's "Project verses from bible.com" extension in the laptop's Chrome,
**Screens → bible.com windows…** chooses which of its windows each language goes to. A
verse you project from bible.com then shows on Norless's projectors, the stage monitor
and phones following along too, in each window's language. **Back to the playlist** in
the live bar (or Next) returns to the song. The live bar also says whether the
extension is connected.

![A verse on a projector](../screenshots/screen-projector-verse.png)

## Everything at once on a big monitor

Once switched on, a laptop window at least 1920 pixels wide (a Full HD monitor, or
larger) opens the playlist in **Big screen**, unless you picked another layout there:
the playlist, the live song with its controls, a live preview of every screen of the
room, a tile for each musician's and vocalist's phone or tablet showing what it shows
now, and the time, service or rehearsal, the end, the problems and who's here.

![Big screen on a Full HD monitor](../screenshots/fhd-big-screen.png)

- A wider window shows more, at the same text size: on a QHD monitor the band's devices
  get a column of their own, and on a 4K monitor at 100% the next song's whole text does
  too. A 4K monitor scaled to 150% is as wide as a QHD one.
- Click a preview to see it larger: a screen's settings, or for the stage monitor the
  message to the stage, are there. Looking never changes what's live, and the keys
  still move the slides behind it.
- A narrower window shows the Controller, and the layout menu offers Big screen again
  once the window is wide enough.

## The welcome page

Once switched on, the Pages menu has **Welcome** first: the projectors show the
community's name and logo, the time left until the next service or rehearsal, and the
announcements an owner writes in Settings › Pages, one after another. The countdown goes
at the start; the rest stays until something goes live.

![The welcome page on a projector](../screenshots/screen-projector-welcome.png)
