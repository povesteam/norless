# Offline at camp

A laptop that kept the songs projects them without internet: at a camp, a retreat, a
hall without wifi.

## Before you leave

On the laptop, with internet, open your community, then **My account** (your photo at
the top right), and in its **This device** tab switch on **Keep <community>'s songs for
offline**. It says how many songs it kept and when; it keeps them again when songs
change. Install Norless on that laptop (the
browser's install button), so it opens without internet.

## Without internet

![The offline page](../screenshots/laptop-offline.png)

Open Norless: the **Offline** notice in the corner has **Open what this device kept**.
That page has the newest playlists and the songs.

- **A new playlist**: name it, then add songs by searching their title or a few words
  of their text.
- **Project**: opens the projector window on the second screen. Tap a song to show it;
  Previous, Blank and Next move through it, and so do the clicker and the arrow keys.

Playlists made offline stay on that laptop. Nothing is sent back when the internet
returns.
