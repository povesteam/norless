# For owners

The settings are in the menu (Classic) or the community's bar, for owners only.

![Settings](../screenshots/laptop-settings.png)

## Members and roles

Invite people by email in **Settings → Members**. Their first login with that address
accepts the invitation.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| (none) | Read everything, see who is online, like songs, send ideas. |
| **Team** | Control what's live, change playlists, add chords and tempo, record. |
| **Editor** | Write and correct songs, create and delete them. |
| **Owner** | Everything, including the members and the settings. |

Roles combine: a worship leader may be team and editor.

![Members](../screenshots/laptop-members.png)

## The features

**More → Features** shows everything Norless can do, each feature after the one it
needs: the App menu leads to the laptop layouts, which lead to practice rooms, and so on.
Each feature's color is its branch (Screens, Live, Stage, Chords, Songs, Statistics, App,
Team). A filled feature is on, a ring is off, a lock means it needs another feature
first, and an hourglass means it's planned. Tap one to read what it does.

The community starts like the old Norless (Classic), with every other feature off. Tap
a feature to switch it on or off for everyone; switching on one that needs others
switches them on too. Switch a few on at a time, after a Sunday that went well. People
who open the app after a change see what's new. New features arrive switched off,
marked new, until you switch them on.

Members see the same tree, with the planned features, and ask you for one there. The
**Ideas** page lists what they asked for.

![Feature tree](../screenshots/laptop-features.png)

## Screens and pages

- **Screens**: a projector, a musicians' tablet, the stage TV or the live
  stream's overlay, each with its languages, look and own link. A TV can be paired with
  a code instead of typing its link.
- **Pages**: a start page before the service and an end page after it (any https
  address), projected from the **Pages** button.
- **Theme**: the community's colors, font and logo.

## Schedule

**Settings → Schedule** has the weekly services and rehearsals, and one-off events.
Norless uses it to mark what was sung in a service and what in a rehearsal, to estimate
when a playlist ends, and to open next week's playlist. Rehearsals use the next
service's playlist: they're there to learn its songs.

Once switched on, paste the church's calendar there too (its iCal address, such as Google
Calendar's "Secret address in iCal format"): members see what's coming in it. Only
owners see the address.

## Ideas and song feedback

Members send ideas from the menu; owners read them under **Ideas and feedback**. With
song feedback switched on, **Settings → Song feedback** lists the songs members dislike, with their
reasons, and the songs taken out of use. **Exclude** takes a song out with a reason:
search no longer finds it, and its page says why.
