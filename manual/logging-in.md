# Logging in

Norless has no passwords.

![The login page](../screenshots/laptop-login.png)

## With Google, or an email link

- **Log in with Google** uses your Google account's email.
- **Email me a login link** sends a link that works once, for 15 minutes. Norless never
  says whether an address belongs to a member.

You stay logged in for 30 days on that device. **My account**, which your photo opens,
has its parts in tabs. Under **Logins** it lists where you're logged in (the browser and
when it was last used), logs out one of those places from here, and logs you out here
or everywhere (a lost phone, for example). Under **This device** it corrects whether
it's a phone, a tablet or a laptop.

![Where you're logged in](../screenshots/laptop-account-logins.png)

Only people the owners invited can edit or control. Anyone else who logs in sees what
a visitor sees: songs and playlists, and what's live.

## From your phone

On a shared laptop, you don't need to type anything:

1. On the laptop's login page, find **Log in with your phone**: a QR code, a short
   code and a two-digit number.
2. Scan the code with your phone, where you're logged in. Check that the number is
   the same.
3. Choose:
   - **Log in as me**: the laptop is yours, like any login.
   - **As the community's laptop**: the laptop gets the team role, only in your
     community, until its browser closes or for 12 hours. Changes show as
     "Laptop (your name)".
   - **As a guest musician**: for a visiting musician's phone, for 4 hours.

When your phone and the laptop are on the same wifi, the phone offers "Log in the
laptop?" by itself, without scanning.

Visitors don't need to log in at all: the songs, the playlists and what's live are
public, and **anyone's phone can follow the live song** at your community's address
.
