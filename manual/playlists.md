# Playlists

For the team, who prepare Sunday's songs.

![An empty playlist](../screenshots/laptop-empty-playlist.png)

## Making Sunday's playlist

1. Open the menu at the top left and press **New playlist**. Its date names it (the next
   service's, when the community has a schedule); click it to add a name, like "Evening",
   which shows before the date.
2. Add songs: type part of a title or a line of the lyrics, anywhere on the page, and
   press Enter, or click a result. Diacritics don't matter: "isus e domn" finds
   "Isus e Domn".
3. Click the empty search box to see random songs, when you look for ideas. Once
   likes are on, the songs you like come first.
4. Drag a row to move it. On a phone or tablet, long-press first.

Thirty minutes after a service ends, Norless opens next week's playlist by itself.

![Searching](../screenshots/laptop-search.png)

## Dividers and Bible references

- Type "Predica" and choose **Add divider** to put a heading in the playlist, for the
  sermon for example. A divider never goes live.
- **Add divider above**, in an entry's actions (right-click, or the ⋯ button), puts one
  just above it.
- Type a reference such as "Ioan 3:16-18" to add a Bible reading; the projectors show
  the reference.

Once switched on, a divider or a text slide can have planned minutes, and every entry shows
when it starts. The playlist shows its estimated end, and how late it runs.

## Slides from files

Once switched on, **Slides from a file**, beside the search box, adds a PDF or pictures
(PNG, JPEG, WebP), up to 100 MB and 300 pages. Dropping files onto the playlist adds them
where they land. Each PDF becomes an entry, named after its file; pictures picked together become one entry, a page
each, in their names' order.

![A playlist with slides from a file](../screenshots/laptop-slides.png)

- The entry shows **Preparing** while Norless draws its pages, then its first page and how
  many it has. It goes live like a song: Next and Previous, the keys and a clicker move
  through the pages, then to the next entry; its pages show where a song's parts do.
- A presentation is added as a PDF, which looks exactly as designed: PowerPoint, File →
  Save As → PDF; Keynote, File → Export To → PDF; Google Slides and Canva, Download → PDF.
  Dropping a PowerPoint or Keynote file says so and adds nothing.

![A PowerPoint file dropped onto the playlist](../screenshots/laptop-slides-as-pdf.png)
- In the entry's actions: **Add a file for Ukrainian** (or the community's other
  languages), shown on that language's screens instead; **Next page every … seconds**, which
  loops through the pages, for announcements before the service, until anything else
  goes live; **Download the file**; **Edit** to rename it.

## Print, save, copy

The playlist's ⋯ menu, next to its title, has **Print**, **Save as HTML** (a page that
works offline) and **Copy as text**, with every refrain written out where it's sung.
Singers use them to learn the songs.

Once switched on, the same menu has **YouTube chapters**: after the service, type when the
first song starts in the video, and copy the chapters into its description.

## Host's words

**Host's words**, in an entry's actions, keeps what the host says around it, in each
language. The host follows them in the **Host** view on a phone or tablet: what's live
with its words in large text, and what comes next.
