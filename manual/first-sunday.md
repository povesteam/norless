# Your first Sunday

For the operator at the laptop, with Classic.

![The playlist and its slides](../screenshots/laptop-playlist.png)

## Before the service

1. Log in on the laptop (see [Logging in](logging-in.md)). From your phone is quickest.
2. Open the menu at the top left and pick Sunday's playlist.
3. Press **Project** for each projector. The projector opens full screen on the second
   display. The first time, the browser asks to allow it; say yes. Without that, a
   window opens: drag it to the projector.
4. Pressing **Project** again brings the same window back. It never opens a second one.

One playlist drives every language: the Romanian projector shows Romanian, the
Ukrainian one Ukrainian.

## During the service

- **Click** an entry to look at its slides. Nothing changes on the screens.
- **Go live** (or double-click the entry, or Enter) sends its first slide live.
- **Click a slide** to send that slide live.
- **Page Down** or **→** goes to the next slide, and into the next song at the end.
  **Page Up** or **←** goes back.
- **Esc** blanks the screens, during a prayer for example. **Show again** brings back
  the same slide.
- The bar at the bottom has **Blank**, **Previous** and **Next** too, and **Pages**
  for the start and end pages.

These keys also work while the projector's window has the focus.

A clicker or a Bluetooth remote can move the slides once **Media keys** is switched on
in the live bar.

## When something goes wrong

- **A song is missing:** type its name anywhere on the page (nothing needs to be
  focused) and press Enter. It is added at the end of the playlist.
- **The wrong song went live:** press Esc, then send the right one live.
- **The connection drops:** the screens keep the last slide. The page shows
  "Reconnecting…" and reconnects by itself.
- **A typo on a slide:** editors can press the pencil, fix it and save; the screens
  show the fix at once.

## After the service

Nothing to do. Norless keeps which songs were sung, for the statistics and for the
times it estimates next Sunday.

## On a phone

The menu is at the left of the top bar and your photo at the right, which opens My
account. While a song is live, its title and part show above Previous, Blank and Next.
